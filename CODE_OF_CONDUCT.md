# Code of Conduct

Echo is an anonymous microblogging service. People use it to speak without handing over a legal identity. That only works if participation stays open to everyone and if the software is not used to harm people.

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, review discussion, and to deployments of Echo that this team operates. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Echo a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, and healthy community, and to design and operate Echo so that it does not expose people to avoidable harm.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Accept correction, and give it without insults.
- Respect the anonymity model. Do not try to uncover, publish, or demand another person's real identity.
- Use the report and moderation tools for content that breaks this code.
- Keep changes small, reviewable, and honest about what they do to user data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Discrimination, slurs, or exclusion based on any characteristic listed in the pledge.
- Harassment, threats, doxxing, or sexual attention that was not agreed to.
- Publishing another person's private information, including a real name, contact detail, or network address that Echo itself refuses to store.
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks in issues, reviews, or posts when a deployment is under this team's care.
- Using the project to stalk people, distribute malware, or evade a moderation action by abusing anonymity.

## Do no harm

Echo does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, or government identifier. Contributors must not add collection of that data, or of client IP addresses, unless the change is rejected or is accompanied by an explicit privacy review and a documentation update in [docs/PRIVACY.md](docs/PRIVACY.md).

Harmful public content is handled inside the product. A signed-in session can report a post. Open reports can be dismissed, the post can be hidden, or the author session can be banned. Repeated reports from distinct sessions can hide a post automatically. Hidden posts stay out of the public feed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers enforce this code. They may edit or reject contributions, close discussions, hide posts, or ban sessions on a deployment they operate.

Report a violation by opening a GitHub issue on [MBFG9000/echo-backend](https://github.com/MBFG9000/echo-backend), or by contacting a maintainer listed in [OWNERS.md](OWNERS.md). Reports should describe what happened and where. Do not include someone else's private identity in the report.

Maintainers will respond, keep the report as private as the public issue tracker allows, and state the outcome they can share. A maintainer who is the subject of a report does not decide that report.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and to public spaces when a person is representing Echo or this team. Representation includes using an official project channel, posting from an official account, or acting as a maintainer at an event.
